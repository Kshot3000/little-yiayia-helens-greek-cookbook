Welcome to Yiayia's Kitchen

Helen was born in Chicago and raised by her mother and father who came to Chicago from Greece. This cookbook preserves the flavors, stories, and love she passed down through generations, with inspiration from Crete, Santorini, Corfu and beyond.

From Our Family to Yours

Each recipe here was handwritten, tested, and loved in Helen's kitchen on the South Side of Chicago. Made with simple ingredients, a lot of olive oil, and always with family around the table.

  • Family-tested recipes from Chicago and the islands
  • Stories behind each dish
  • Measurements in cups and spoons, just like Yiayia used
  • Greek flag colors and island inspiration throughout

Island Roots

Yiayia's parents spoke of blue seas, whitewashed villages, and the scent of oregano on the wind. We honor those memories with recipes inspired by Crete, the Cyclades, the Ionian Islands, and the Dodecanese.

Crete

Olive oil, dakos, and hearty mountain flavors.

Santorini

Fava, cherry tomatoes, and sea breezes.

Corfu

Pastitsada and citrus bright dishes.

Rhodes

Sweet loukoumi and spiced meats.
