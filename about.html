About Yiayia Helen

Helen was born in Chicago and raised by her mother and father who came to Chicago from Greece. Growing up on the South Side, she learned to cook at her mother's side, translating old-world flavors with ingredients she could find in Chicago markets.

Yiayia was known in the neighborhood as “Little Yiayia” — petite in stature, enormous in heart. Her kitchen was always open, her pantry always stocked with olive oil, dried oregano, and lemons.

She taught her children and grandchildren to roll spanakopita, simmer avgolemono, and always, always taste before serving. This cookbook is a tribute to her legacy.

“Food is love you can eat.” — Yiayia Helen

Chicago & Greece

Helen's story is the classic Greek-American story: parents who crossed the ocean, a daughter born in Chicago, and a kitchen that became a bridge between two worlds. The blue and white of the Greek flag lives in her tablecloths, her recipes, and the stories she told.

Family Traditions

Sunday spanakopita, Christmas melomakarona, and summer tomatoes.

Chicago Roots

South Side markets, Greek festivals, and neighbor kitchens.
