Appetizers & Salads

Tzatziki

Story: Always served with warm pita. Yiayia said it should be thick and creamy.

  • 2 cups Greek yogurt
  • 1 cucumber, grated and drained
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice
  • Fresh dill, salt

Greek Salad — Horiatiki

Story: No lettuce — just tomatoes, cucumbers, olives and feta. The island way.

  • 3 ripe tomatoes, wedged
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 red onion, thin
  • Kalamata olives
  • Feta block, olive oil, oregano

Dolmades — Grape Leaf Rolls

Story: Yiayia's mother rolled these every summer with fresh leaves from the yard.

  • 1 jar grape leaves
  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 lb ground lamb
  • Parsley, lemon juice, olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, allspice

Taramosalata

Story: A bright pink spread from the Aegean, served with crusty bread.

  • 1/2 cup tarama (fish roe)
  • 2 slices white bread, soaked
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Dill for garnish

Soups & Stews

Avgolemono Soup

Story: Made when someone was under the weather. Always served hot.

  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 2 eggs beaten with 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Fasolada — White Bean Stew

Story: Called the "Greek national dish" — slow-simmered beans with vegetables.

  • 2 cups dried white beans, soaked
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • Olive oil, oregano, bay leaf

Giouvetsi — Lamb with Orzo

Story: Sunday dinner staple. Tender lamb braised with orzo pasta.

  • 2 lbs lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 1 cup orzo pasta
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • Cinnamon stick, allspice
  • Kefalotyri cheese on top

Main Dishes

Moussaka

Story: Yiayia's father's favorite. Layers of eggplant, potatoes, and rich béchamel.

  • 2 eggplants, sliced
  • 2 potatoes, sliced
  • 1 lb ground lamb or beef
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • Béchamel sauce with nutmeg

Spanakopita

Story: Sunday mornings, phyllo on the counter, Yiayia humming while rolling.

  • 1 lb fresh spinach, chopped
  • 1 cup feta, crumbled
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 8 sheets phyllo dough

Briam — Greek Vegetable Bake

Story: Summer tomatoes from the garden, roasted slow.

  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 eggplants, cubed
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • Olive oil, oregano, salt

Souvlaki — Grilled Pork Skewers

Story: Summer grilling, the whole neighborhood could smell it.

  • 1.5 lbs pork shoulder, cubed
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Lemon juice, oregano
  • Serve with pita, tzatziki, tomato

Stifado — Beef Stew

Story: Slow-cooked beef with pearl onions and warm spices.

  • 2 lbs beef chuck, cubed
  • 1 lb pearl onions, peeled
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Cinnamon, bay leaf, red wine

Fasolakia — Green Beans in Tomato Sauce

Story: Simple, rustic, and always on the table in summer.

  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • Olive oil, oregano
  • Salt, pepper

Desserts & Sweets

Galaktoboureko

Story: Holiday dessert, semolina custard wrapped in phyllo, soaked in syrup.

  • 6 sheets phyllo
  • 1 cup semolina
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • Lemon zest for syrup

Baklava

Story: Crispy layers of phyllo and walnuts, drenched in honey syrup.

  • 1 lb phyllo dough
  • 2 cups walnuts, chopped
  • 1 cup butter, melted
  • Cinnamon, cloves
  • Honey-lemon syrup

Koulourakia — Butter Cookies

Story: Yiayia baked these for Easter and every celebration.

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg, vanilla, orange zest
  • Poppy seeds for rolling

Greek Yogurt with Honey

Story: Simple, thick, and drizzled with mountain honey.

  • 2 cups thick Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • Cinnamon
  • Walnuts or pistachios

Island Inspiration

Yiayia's parents came from Greece and spoke often of the islands. These flavors traveled with them to Chicago.

Cretan Olive Oil

Robust, peppery oil from Crete. The base of Yiayia's kitchen.

Santorini Fava

Smooth yellow split pea puree with lemon and olive oil, sunny like the Cyclades.

Corfu Pastitsada

Tomato-based meat sauce with cinnamon, a taste of the Ionian.

Rhodes Loukoumi

Soft Turkish delight, sweet memories of harbor walks.
