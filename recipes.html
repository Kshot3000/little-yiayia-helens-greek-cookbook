Appetizers & Salads

Tzatziki

🥒

Story: Always served with warm pita. Yiayia said it should be thick and creamy.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Greek yogurt
  • 1 cucumber, grated and drained
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Fresh dill, salt

Instructions

  1. Grate cucumber and squeeze out excess water in a clean kitchen towel
  2. Mix yogurt, cucumber, garlic, olive oil, and lemon juice in a bowl
  3. Season with salt and chopped dill
  4. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving
  5. Serve chilled with pita bread or grilled meats

Greek Salad — Horiatiki

🥗

Story: No lettuce — just tomatoes, cucumbers, olives and feta. The island way.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe tomatoes, wedged
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup Kalamata olives
  • 1 block feta cheese
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, oregano

Instructions

  1. Place tomatoes, cucumber, and onion in a large bowl
  2. Add olives and crumble feta on top
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle oregano
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste
  5. Serve immediately, no dressing needed beyond oil

Dolmades — Grape Leaf Rolls

🍃

Story: Yiayia's mother rolled these every summer with fresh leaves from the yard.

Ingredients

  • 1 jar grape leaves
  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 lb ground lamb
  • 1/2 cup chopped parsley
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, allspice

Instructions

  1. Rinse grape leaves and pat dry
  2. Mix rice, lamb, parsley, lemon juice, and spices
  3. Place 1 tbsp filling on each leaf, roll tightly
  4. Place rolls seam-side down in pot
  5. Pour olive oil and water over top, simmer 45 minutes
  6. Cool before serving, drizzle with lemon

Taramosalata

🧂

Story: A bright pink spread from the Aegean, served with crusty bread.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup tarama (fish roe)
  • 2 slices white bread, soaked and squeezed
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh dill for garnish

Instructions

  1. Soak bread in water, squeeze out thoroughly
  2. Place tarama and bread in food processor
  3. Add lemon juice and olive oil gradually while blending
  4. Blend until smooth and creamy pink color
  5. Refrigerate 30 minutes before serving
  6. Garnish with dill and serve with bread

Soups & Stews

Avgolemono Soup

🍲

Story: Made when someone was under the weather. Always served hot.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Bring chicken broth to simmer, add rice and cook 15 minutes
  2. In separate bowl, whisk eggs and lemon juice until frothy
  3. Slowly add hot broth to egg mixture, whisking constantly
  4. Return mixture to pot, do not boil
  5. Season with salt and pepper
  6. Serve immediately while hot

Fasolada — White Bean Stew

🫘

Story: Called the "Greek national dish" — slow-simmered beans with vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups dried white beans, soaked overnight
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Oregano, bay leaf, salt

Instructions

  1. Drain soaked beans and place in large pot
  2. Add onion, carrots, tomatoes, olive oil, and herbs
  3. Cover with water, bring to boil
  4. Reduce heat and simmer 2-3 hours until tender
  5. Stir occasionally, add water as needed
  6. Season with salt and serve hot

Giouvetsi — Lamb with Orzo

🍖

Story: Sunday dinner staple. Tender lamb braised with orzo pasta.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 1 cup orzo pasta
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Allspice, salt, pepper
  • Kefalotyri cheese

Instructions

  1. Brown lamb in olive oil until seared on all sides
  2. Add tomatoes, spices, and water to cover
  3. Simmer 1.5 hours until lamb is tender
  4. Cook orzo separately according to package
  5. Mix orzo with lamb sauce
  6. Top with grated kefalotyri cheese before serving

Main Dishes

Moussaka

🍆

Story: Yiayia's father's favorite. Layers of eggplant, potatoes, and rich béchamel.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggplants, sliced
  • 2 potatoes, sliced
  • 1 lb ground lamb
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup béchamel sauce
  • Nutmeg, salt, pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F
  2. Salt eggplant slices, let sit 30 min, then pat dry
  3. Fry eggplant and potatoes until golden
  4. Brown lamb with tomatoes, season with spices
  5. Layer eggplant, potatoes, and meat in baking dish
  6. Top with béchamel, bake 45 minutes
  7. Cool 10 minutes before serving

Spanakopita

🥧

Story: Sunday mornings, phyllo on the counter, Yiayia humming while rolling.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh spinach, chopped
  • 1 cup feta, crumbled
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 8 sheets phyllo dough
  • 3 eggs, beaten

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Sauté spinach until wilted, cool completely
  3. Mix spinach with feta, onions, eggs, and olive oil
  4. Lay phyllo sheets on baking dish, brush with oil
  5. Spread spinach mixture evenly
  6. Fold phyllo over top, brush with more oil
  7. Bake 40 minutes until golden brown

Briam — Greek Vegetable Bake

🥒

Story: Summer tomatoes from the garden, roasted slow.

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini, sliced
  • 2 eggplants, cubed
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Oregano, salt, pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F
  2. Arrange vegetables in baking dish in layers
  3. Drizzle with olive oil
  4. Season with oregano, salt, and pepper
  5. Bake 45-60 minutes until vegetables are tender
  6. Stir occasionally during baking
  7. Serve warm or at room temperature

Souvlaki — Grilled Pork Skewers

🍢

Story: Summer grilling, the whole neighborhood could smell it.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs pork shoulder, cubed
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 2 tbsp oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Mix pork with garlic, oil, lemon juice, and oregano
  2. Marinate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight
  3. Thread onto skewers
  4. Grill over medium heat 8-10 minutes, turning
  5. Cook until internal temperature reaches 145°F
  6. Serve with pita, tzatziki, and tomato

Stifado — Beef Stew

🍲

Story: Slow-cooked beef with pearl onions and warm spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs beef chuck, cubed
  • 1 lb pearl onions, peeled
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • Bay leaf, red wine

Instructions

  1. Brown beef cubes in olive oil
  2. Add onions, garlic, and cinnamon stick
  3. Pour in tomatoes and red wine
  4. Bring to simmer, cover, and cook 2 hours
  5. Stir occasionally, add water if needed
  6. Remove bay leaf and cinnamon before serving
  7. Serve with rice or crusty bread

Fasolakia — Green Beans in Tomato Sauce

🫛

Story: Simple, rustic, and always on the table in summer.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Oregano, salt, pepper

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion in olive oil until translucent
  2. Add green beans and cook 5 minutes
  3. Pour in crushed tomatoes
  4. Season with oregano, salt, and pepper
  5. Simmer covered 30-40 minutes until tender
  6. Stir occasionally during cooking
  7. Serve warm as a side dish

Desserts & Sweets

Galaktoboureko

🍮

Story: Holiday dessert, semolina custard wrapped in phyllo, soaked in syrup.

Ingredients

  • 6 sheets phyllo dough
  • 1 cup semolina
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 cup butter, melted
  • Lemon zest for syrup

Instructions

  1. Make custard: heat milk, add semolina slowly while whisking
  2. Cook until thick, add sugar and cool
  3. Layer phyllo with butter between each sheet
  4. Spread custard in center
  5. Fold phyllo over custard, seal edges
  6. Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes until golden
  7. Pour hot syrup over hot pastry, cool completely

Baklava

🍯

Story: Crispy layers of phyllo and walnuts, drenched in honey syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb phyllo dough
  • 2 cups walnuts, chopped
  • 1 cup butter, melted
  • Cinnamon and cloves
  • 1 cup honey, 1/2 cup water

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Mix walnuts with cinnamon and cloves
  3. Lay 8 phyllo sheets in pan, brushing each with butter
  4. Spread 1/3 walnut mixture
  5. Repeat layers twice more
  6. Cut into diamond shapes before baking
  7. Bake 50 minutes until golden
  8. Boil honey and water 10 minutes, pour over hot baklava

Koulourakia — Butter Cookies

🍪

Story: Yiayia baked these for Easter and every celebration.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • Vanilla, orange zest
  • Poppy seeds

Instructions

  1. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy
  2. Add egg, vanilla, and orange zest
  3. Gradually add flour until dough forms
  4. Divide dough, roll into ropes 1/2 inch thick
  5. Twist ropes into traditional shape
  6. Brush with egg wash, sprinkle poppy seeds
  7. Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes until golden
  8. Cool on wire rack before serving

Greek Yogurt with Honey

🥛

Story: Simple, thick, and drizzled with mountain honey.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • Cinnamon to taste
  • Walnuts or pistachios

Instructions

  1. Spoon yogurt into serving bowls
  2. Drizzle honey generously over top
  3. Sprinkle with cinnamon
  4. Add chopped walnuts or pistachios
  5. Serve immediately or chill for later
  6. Best served with fresh fruit on the side
